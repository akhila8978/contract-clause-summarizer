ACME CORP — INFORMATION SECURITY POLICY (v3.2)

1. Data Protection. All customer data must be encrypted at rest using AES-256 and in transit using TLS 1.2 or higher. Vendors handling Acme data must comply with ISO 27001 and SOC 2 Type II.

2. Breach Notification. Any vendor must notify Acme of a confirmed security incident within 24 hours of detection, in writing, including scope and affected systems.

3. Audit Rights. Acme reserves the right to conduct, with 30 days' notice, an annual security audit of vendor environments handling Acme data.

4. Subprocessors. Vendors must obtain written approval before engaging any subprocessor that will process Acme data.

5. Data Retention & Deletion. On termination, all Acme data must be returned or securely destroyed within 30 days, with written certification.

6. Personnel. Vendor personnel with access to Acme systems must complete annual security training and pass background checks.
