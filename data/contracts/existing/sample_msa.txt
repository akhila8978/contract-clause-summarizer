MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT — Acme Corp ("Customer") and BlueOps Pvt Ltd ("Vendor")
Effective Date: 1 January 2024.

1. PARTIES. Customer: Acme Corp, Bangalore. Vendor: BlueOps Pvt Ltd, Hyderabad.

2. TERM. Initial term 24 months from Effective Date, auto-renewing for 12-month periods unless either party gives 30 days' written notice prior to renewal.

3. PAYMENT. Customer shall pay Vendor INR 4,80,000 per quarter, within 45 days of invoice. Late payments accrue interest at 1.5% per month.

4. SLA. Vendor commits to Sev-1 response within 30 minutes and resolution within 8 hours. Service credits not applicable.

5. CONFIDENTIALITY. Each party shall protect the other's confidential information for 2 years following termination.

6. DATA PROTECTION. Vendor will use industry-standard encryption.

7. SECURITY INCIDENT NOTIFICATION. Vendor shall notify Customer of confirmed security incidents within 72 hours.

8. AUDIT. Customer may audit Vendor with 90 days' prior notice, not more than once every 24 months.

9. SUBPROCESSORS. Vendor may engage subprocessors at its discretion.

10. LIABILITY. Vendor's aggregate liability is capped at fees paid in the prior 6 months.

11. TERMINATION. Either party may terminate for material breach with 30 days' cure period.

12. GOVERNING LAW. Laws of Karnataka, India.
